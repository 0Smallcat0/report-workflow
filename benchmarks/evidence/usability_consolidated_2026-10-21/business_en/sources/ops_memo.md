# Support operations memo, Q2 2026

This memo and the attached ticket sheet use illustrative figures prepared for a training exercise.

## Context

The support team runs three channels: email, chat and phone. Chat was moved to a 7x24 schedule in April 2026; email and phone are staffed on weekdays only.

## What changed in June

- Two email agents moved to the chat queue at the start of June to cover the evening shift.
- A billing change on 3 June produced a spike of email questions about invoices.

## Questions for the review

1. Which channel is under the most pressure, and is it getting worse?
2. Should the two agents return to the email queue?

## Known gaps

- Customer satisfaction comes from a post-ticket survey; fewer than one in five customers answer it.
- Ticket counts do not separate new customers from existing ones.
